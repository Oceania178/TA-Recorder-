        1. What this app does

        • It records your calls on this laptop: your microphone (left) and everything you hear (right).
        • To write the transcript, the speech is sent to Gemini (Google).
        • Only the transcript text goes to Solace, and only to the Solace address you set in step 4.
        • The audio never goes to Solace. It stays on this laptop and is deleted for good after 3 days.
        • Uninstalling deletes your recordings and the app's settings, including your Gemini key.

        Calls are transcribed by Google Gemini. Tell the candidate at the start of the call that it is recorded.

        Nothing can be recorded until you press "I understand".

        2. Microphone permission

        Windows must let desktop apps use the microphone.

        In Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone, turn on "Microphone access" and "Let desktop apps access your microphone". Then press "Test the microphone" again.

        3. Sound check

        Put on your headset. Press Start, say a few words, then play the beep: both bars must move.

        You (mic) · left
        What you hear (system audio) · right

        Left bar still: check the headset mic is plugged in and selected in Windows. Right bar still: the beep plays on Windows' default output device — make it your headset (Windows Sound settings).

        4. Solace address

        Enter the Solace address your team uses. The app sends transcripts only there.

        The real test: in Solace, press "Call & record" on a role-play candidate. When the browser asks to open TA Call Recorder, tick "Always allow" and open it — the app should start recording.

        5. 3CX dials for "Call & record" (can be skipped)

        Make 3CX the app that opens phone links: Windows Settings → Apps → Default apps → search "TEL" → choose 3CX. If you call with another app (Zalo PC, Teams…), skip this step and use "Record only, I'll call myself" in Solace.
